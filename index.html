01 / ABOUT

A quantitative approach to biological questions.

I am an Associate Professor with Habilitation at the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon. My research is centred on bacterial evolution and the emergence and spread of antibiotic resistance.

My work combines laboratory experimentation with computational and mathematical models to investigate how genetic interactions, horizontal gene transfer and ecological context shape evolutionary outcomes.

A complementary line of research explores the human and psychological factors associated with antibiotic use, connecting microbiology to broader public-health questions.
